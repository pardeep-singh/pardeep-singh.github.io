Engineer / Team lead / Runner

I build machine learning systems, and the teams that ship them.

I have been writing software professionally since 2015—first backend services and APIs, then NLP, and for the last several years production ML, LLM pipelines and agentic systems.

I am currently the Engineering Lead at Srotas Health, where I lead a cross-functional team of six engineers and work with the founders on engineering strategy. We build AI for healthcare: an agentic system that evaluates clinical criteria across 1B+ patient records in under ten seconds, LLM-backed search, and document pipelines for patient feasibility assessment.

Before that I spent a few months as an independent AI architect, and three and a half years at Fairmatic, where I led the machine learning engineering team, modernised the insurance risk models, and built the ML deployment platform. Earlier, I worked on conversational AI at Sense, went from backend to ML engineering at Helpshift, and started out at Paxcel Technologies. The details are on the experience page.

Outside of work I contribute to open source—most recently 31 merged pull requests to OpenMed, a local-first healthcare NLP toolkit, and fixes to Arize's Phoenix and OpenInference. On Kaggle I am an Expert in both Datasets and Notebooks; my cricket datasets have been downloaded over 15,000 times, and I write notebooks on fine-tuning small LLMs. See projects.

I like teaching what I learn. I ran an internal machine learning classroom at Helpshift, organised 25+ weekly technical sessions at Fairmatic, and hosted a study group for the Hugging Face Agents course with my cohort from the Advanced Deep Learning programme at IISc Bangalore.

Away from the keyboard, I am a distance runner. I have run more than twenty half marathons and am training for my first full marathon. You can follow along on Strava.

I hold a Master's in Computer Applications from Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology and a Bachelor's from Panjab University. I live in Chandigarh, India, and work remotely.
